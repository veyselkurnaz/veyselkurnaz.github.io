Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 16, 2026

At Medieval Dice, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our mobile application.

1. Data Collection and Usage

While we do not collect personal identifiers like your name or email address directly, our app collects certain technical and gameplay data to function properly:

  • Game Progress: Your game progress, scores, and unlocked items are stored securely via Apple Game Center / Google Play Games services.
  • Anonymous Analytics: We collect device type, operating system version, crash reports, and general usage statistics to improve the game's stability and experience.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Our app is supported by advertising. We use third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob) which may collect and use your device advertising identifier (like IDFA on iOS or Advertising ID on Android) to deliver personalized ads.

On iOS devices, ad tracking is strictly governed by Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. You will be prompted to grant or deny permission for ad tracking. You can change these preferences anytime in your device settings.

3. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information without consent, please contact us.

4. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

You have the right to request the deletion of your data. If you wish to delete your game progress or any associated data collected by our app, you can do so by contacting us directly at the email provided below, or by managing your Game Center / Play Games data via your device settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding your data, data deletion, or our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at kurnazbusiness@gmail.com.
